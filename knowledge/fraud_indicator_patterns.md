# Fraud Indicator Patterns

## Indicator 1 - Early Claim

A claim submitted shortly after policy inception may require
additional investigation.

## Indicator 2 - Near Limit Amount

A claim amount very close to the policy limit is an investigation
indicator.

## Indicator 3 - Missing Information

Missing loss dates, amounts, policy information, or supporting
documents should trigger additional review.

## Indicator 4 - Delayed Reporting

A significant delay between the loss date and claim reporting may
require additional investigation.

## Indicator 5 - Repeated Similar Claims

A claim that is substantially similar to a previous claim may require
review of historical claim information.

## Indicator 6 - Inconsistent Information

Inconsistent dates, claim types, policy information, or amounts
should be investigated before approval.
