# Underwriting Rules

## Rule 1 - Early Policy Claims

Claims filed within 7 days of the policy inception date should be
flagged for additional review.

## Rule 2 - Near Policy Limit

Claims whose requested amount is within 2% of the applicable policy
coverage limit should be flagged for investigator review.

## Rule 3 - Excessive Claim Amount

A claim amount greater than the applicable policy coverage limit
cannot be automatically approved.

## Rule 4 - Policy Period

The loss date must fall within the policy effective period.
Claims outside the policy period require manual review or rejection
depending on the coverage determination.

## Rule 5 - Missing Critical Information

Claims missing required information such as policy number, claim ID,
loss date, claim amount, or claim type must not be automatically
approved.

## Rule 6 - Delayed Reporting

Claims reported more than 7 days after the loss date should be flagged
for additional review.

## Rule 7 - Duplicate Claims

Duplicate claim IDs must be investigated before processing.
