# Coverage Guidelines

## Property Claims

Homeowners policies may cover property damage caused by covered
events such as water damage, fire, and theft when the event is within
the policy coverage period and applicable coverage exists.

## Coverage Limit

The requested claim amount must not exceed the applicable policy
coverage limit for automatic approval.

## Policy Period

Coverage applies only when the loss occurs between the policy
effective date and policy expiration date.

## Water Damage

Water damage claims should be checked against the property's
applicable water damage coverage.

## Fire

Fire claims require verification that the loss occurred during the
active policy period.

## Theft

Theft claims require verification of policy coverage and may require
additional documentation such as a police report or inventory of
stolen property.
